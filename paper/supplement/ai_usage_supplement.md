# Supplementary Material: Use of AI Coding Assistants

This supplement accompanies the CMP263 paper *"Does the text of an agent skill predict its replication on GitHub? A predictive study on GitSkills"* (Appendix B). We used AI coding assistants to guide and support the execution of the work, including writing and running the code, and drafting and revising the text. The prompts below are a selection focused on methodological guidance and on the execution of the methods; they were written in Portuguese and are summarized here in English, with related prompts grouped together. The authors defined the research question and the objective, approved every methodological decision and are responsible for the whole work.

## Methodological guidance

1. "Help us structure the research protocol in phases, aligned with the assignment, for our approval before any model is trained."
2. "Propose a split by author for the test set, and explain its implications."
3. "Suggest how to evaluate the models: the validation strategy, nested cross-validation for hyperparameter tuning, the metrics and the statistical tests for comparing models."
4. "Discuss whether the repository attributes should answer a research question of their own or serve as a control analysis."
5. "Review whether the methodology meets the requirements of the assignment."

## Execution of the methods

6. "Implement and run each phase of the approved protocol: exploratory analysis, preprocessing, spot-checking, nested tuning, final models, single holdout evaluation and interpretation."
7. "Prepare the Colab notebook and the automated tests that reproduce the results reported in the paper."

## Writing support

8. "Revise the text of the paper for clarity and cohesion."
