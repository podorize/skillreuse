# 6. Conclusion and future work

This work investigated whether observable characteristics of a skill and of its repository are associated with cross-owner reuse in GitSkills, and how much of this predictability is an artifact of the dataset's construction. Three conclusions hold.

**First**, GitSkills has construction biases that affect predictive studies. The rule that selects the representative makes the probability of it being in `.claude/skills/` grow from 14% to 92% with the number of copies, while the actual share of copies in that folder stays at 5–13%, so location and commit history are spurious predictors of reuse. In addition, 28% of reused skills exist under a single owner and a few accounts concentrate the sample, so counting repositories overstates diffusion and performance depends on a few owners (AUC from 0.615 to 0.747 with the treatment).

**Second**, free of these artifacts, the attributes predict cross-owner reuse with a modest signal confirmed on new data: on the owner-disjoint holdout, the Random Forest reaches an AUC of 0.731 (95% CI 0.716–0.745), and the HistGradientBoosting reduces the RMSLE of `owner_count` by 6% relative to the mean (0.625 against 0.664). Skill and repository contribute complementarily, and the signal is associated with stars and forks, with older last pushes and, in the regression, with the absence of a license. At a decision threshold the balanced accuracy is 0.66 and the precision for single-owner content is 0.28: the model ranks rather than classifies.

**Third**, the limit seems to be in the data and not in the models: six families tie and tuning does not help. The claims are associative and identify neither what makes a skill more or less copied nor its quality.

**Future work.** (i) Model at the copy level, with information prior to reuse, to use attributes of all copies without leakage. (ii) Group near-identical copies to mitigate the fragmentation of the target by exact hash. (iii) Replicate with later snapshots and study the temporal dynamics of diffusion. (iv) Investigate why repositories without a license host skills with more owners. (v) Evaluate text representations within fixed location classes. (vi) Propose to the GitSkills maintainers indicators that separate the representative choice from repository attributes.
