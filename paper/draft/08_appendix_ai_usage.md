# Appendix B — Use of artificial intelligence tools

We used an AI assistant, Claude Code \cite{claude_code}, to answer questions and suggest approaches. Both the authors and the AI assistant wrote and revised the code and the text. All AI-generated content was reviewed by the authors, who are responsible for it. The authors defined the research question and the objective, approved every methodological decision and are responsible for the whole work, including its code, results and conclusions. The prompts used are listed in the supplementary material (`paper/supplement/ai_usage_supplement.md`).
